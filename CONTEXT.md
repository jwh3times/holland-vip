# Holland.VIP Portfolio

Holland.VIP is Jerry Holland's public professional portfolio. It presents his engineering identity,
evidence of past work, current expertise, and ways to connect without exposing confidential employer
or client details.

## Language

### Professional identity

**Portfolio**:
A curated public account of Jerry's professional identity, expertise, and selected work. It is
broader than a résumé because it includes problem-solving narratives, projects, and current
interests.
_Avoid_: Résumé, CV

**Biography**:
The narrative summary of Jerry's career focus, current role, working style, and professional
priorities.
_Avoid_: Profile, overview

**Years of Experience**:
The number of whole years since Jerry began his professional career in 2013. It describes total
career duration rather than time spent in software roles alone.
_Avoid_: Tenure, years coding

**Current Exploration**:
A topic Jerry is actively learning or investigating now, without claiming it as established
expertise.
_Avoid_: Skill, capability

### Expertise

**Skill**:
A named technology, platform, language, tool, or architectural practice with which Jerry has
professional experience.
_Avoid_: Capability, interest

**Technical Capability**:
A repeatable kind of engineering work Jerry can perform, usually drawing on several skills and
practices.
_Avoid_: Skill, technology

### Evidence of work

**Career Highlight**:
A compact, quantified result that shows measurable professional impact.
_Avoid_: Responsibility, achievement

**Technical Achievement**:
An outcome-centered account of a substantial technical effort, supported by several concrete
results or actions.
_Avoid_: Career highlight, project

**Problem-Solving Highlight**:
A concise case study that connects a problem, Jerry's response, and the resulting impact.
_Avoid_: Technical achievement, project description

**Experience**:
A professional role at an employer, identified by title, company, period, location, and notable
responsibilities or outcomes.
_Avoid_: Project, engagement

**Professional Project**:
Selected work completed in a professional role and described in anonymized, generalized terms to
protect employer and client confidentiality.
_Avoid_: Featured repository, open-source project

**Open Source**:
The public-work category that combines curated repositories with Jerry's broader GitHub contribution
activity.
_Avoid_: Professional projects

**Featured Repository**:
A curated public project that Jerry builds or maintains openly on GitHub.
_Avoid_: Professional project, contribution activity

**Contribution Activity**:
Jerry's public GitHub contribution history, presented as evidence of ongoing open-source activity
rather than as a project itself.
_Avoid_: Featured repository, experience

**Education**:
Jerry's formal university degrees and the academic focus or leadership highlights associated with
them.
_Avoid_: Training, certification
